Privacy & Terms

What we collect. Workers: name, suburb, WhatsApp number, skills and, if you allow it, your location. Businesses: business name, WhatsApp number and a PIN. Data is stored with our database provider and shared only as needed to connect workers and jobs (businesses can see worker profiles and contact them on WhatsApp). Chat messages between a business and a worker are deleted when the business marks the job complete.

Your choices. Businesses can delete their account and data inside the app. Workers can delete their account under Profile. Questions: WhatsApp 263781115408.

Terms. BasaNow only connects people. We don't employ workers or guarantee work, payment or identity checks. Agree the price and details before starting, meet safely, and pay directly. Don't post illegal, unsafe or misleading jobs. We may remove accounts that break these rules. BasaNow is a free beta and may change.
